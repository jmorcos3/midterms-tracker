Why 50 is enough for Republicans but Democrats need 51. The Vice President breaks Senate ties, and that is JD Vance. So Republicans keep control at 50 seats; Democrats need an outright 51, a net gain of four.

What a bare majority gets you. Senate: the agenda, every committee chair, and confirmations of judges and cabinet (all simple-majority now). House: the Speaker, committees and subpoena power. Nothing more: ordinary legislation still needs 60 Senate votes to beat a filibuster, and any veto needs two-thirds of both chambers to override, which neither party will have. A Democratic sweep therefore means a blocked White House, not a Democratic agenda.
